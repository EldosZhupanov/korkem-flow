# ADR: descriptive decision title

## Decision

What is being chosen, status and affected scope.

## Context

Observed need and constraints; measurable value of making this decision.

## Alternatives

At least two realistic options, including the existing/simple path when feasible.

## Chosen Option

Selected implementation boundary or mechanism.

## Why

Concrete trade-offs supported by this project's constraints.

## Consequences

What becomes easier/harder; operational and dependency costs.

## Reversal Cost

LOW / MEDIUM / HIGH with the required migration or compensation.

## Kill Condition

What future evidence would make us replace this decision.
